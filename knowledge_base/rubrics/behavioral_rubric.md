# Behavioral Interview Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate behavioral (STAR-method) interview answers on a 1-5 scale.

## Dimensions

### 1. Clarity Score (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Answer is incoherent, rambling, or impossible to follow. No clear structure.
- **2 — Below Average:** Answer has some relevant content but lacks organization. Hard to extract key points.
- **3 — Average:** Answer is understandable but could be more concise and structured. Key points are present but buried.
- **4 — Good:** Answer is well-organized, concise, and easy to follow. Clear beginning, middle, and end.
- **5 — Excellent:** Answer is exceptionally clear, articulate, and precisely structured. Every sentence adds value.

### 2. Depth Score (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Answer is superficial — no specific details, metrics, or examples. Generic statements only.
- **2 — Below Average:** Answer provides some context but lacks specifics. Missing "how" and "why" details.
- **3 — Average:** Answer includes a specific example but missing some important details (metrics, outcomes, learnings).
- **4 — Good:** Answer provides a detailed, specific example with clear context, actions, and results. Includes some metrics or tangible outcomes.
- **5 — Excellent:** Answer is rich with specifics — concrete metrics, named tools/frameworks, clear before/after comparisons, and thoughtful reflection on learnings.

### 3. STAR Adherence (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** No discernible structure. Answer doesn't address Situation, Task, Action, or Result.
- **2 — Below Average:** Mentions situation but jumps to vague actions. Missing clear task definition or results.
- **3 — Average:** Covers most STAR elements but one is significantly weak (usually Result — no concrete outcome stated).
- **4 — Good:** All four STAR elements present and clearly delineated. Result includes a concrete outcome.
- **5 — Excellent:** Perfect STAR execution. Situation is crisp, Task is clear, Actions are specific and personal (uses "I" not "we"), Result includes metrics AND reflection/learning.

### 4. Overall Score (1-5)
Holistic assessment combining clarity, depth, and STAR adherence, plus intangible factors:
- Does the answer feel authentic (not rehearsed or fabricated)?
- Does the candidate demonstrate genuine self-awareness?
- Is there evidence of growth or learning?
- Would this answer make a hiring manager more confident in the candidate?

## Recommendation Logic
- **overall_score < 3** → recommendation: "probe_deeper" — ask a follow-up to help the candidate provide more detail
- **overall_score == 3** → recommendation: "probe_deeper" or "move_on" depending on whether the topic has been sufficiently explored
- **overall_score >= 4** → recommendation: "move_on" or "increase_difficulty" — candidate is strong on this topic
