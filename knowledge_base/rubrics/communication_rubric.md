# Communication Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate communication quality across any interview answer.

## Dimensions

### 1. Clarity Score (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Incoherent or extremely difficult to follow. No logical structure.
- **2 — Below Average:** Understandable in parts but rambling or disorganized overall.
- **3 — Average:** Clear enough to understand the main point, but requires effort from the listener.
- **4 — Good:** Well-articulated and easy to follow. Good use of structure and transitions.
- **5 — Excellent:** Exceptionally clear. Every sentence is intentional. Complex ideas made simple.

### 2. Conciseness (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Extremely verbose. Repeats the same points multiple times. No respect for the interviewer's time.
- **2 — Below Average:** Overly long answers with significant filler content.
- **3 — Average:** Reasonable length but could be tightened. Some redundancy.
- **4 — Good:** Appropriately concise. Covers key points without unnecessary padding.
- **5 — Excellent:** Precisely calibrated length. Dense with information. Not a wasted word.

### 3. Structure (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Stream of consciousness. No beginning, middle, or end.
- **2 — Below Average:** Some attempt at structure but falls apart mid-answer.
- **3 — Average:** Basic structure present. Answer has a start and finish but middle is unorganized.
- **4 — Good:** Clear framework visible. Uses signposting ("First... Second... Finally...").
- **5 — Excellent:** Exceptional structure. Sets up the answer, delivers key points in logical order, and closes with impact.

### 4. Overall Score (1-5)
- Would a listener walk away clearly understanding the candidate's point?
- Did the answer demonstrate respect for the interviewer's time?
- Was the communication style appropriate for a professional setting?
