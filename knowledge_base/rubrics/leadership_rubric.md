# Leadership Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate leadership and management answers on a 1-5 scale.

## Dimensions

### 1. Vision & Strategic Thinking (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** No evidence of forward thinking. Purely reactive mindset.
- **2 — Below Average:** Limited strategic awareness. Focuses only on immediate tasks.
- **3 — Average:** Shows some strategic thinking but stays at a surface level.
- **4 — Good:** Demonstrates clear strategic thinking. Can connect daily work to bigger goals.
- **5 — Excellent:** Exceptional strategic vision. Can articulate long-term direction, anticipate challenges, and think in systems.

### 2. Decision-Making Quality (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** Decisions seem arbitrary. No framework or rationale provided.
- **2 — Below Average:** Some logic but missing important factors. Decision quality is inconsistent.
- **3 — Average:** Reasonable decision-making. Considers key factors but may miss subtleties.
- **4 — Good:** Strong decision-making. Weighs trade-offs, considers stakeholder impact, and articulates rationale.
- **5 — Excellent:** Outstanding decision-making. Considers short-term and long-term implications, addresses uncertainty, and demonstrates judgment maturity.

### 3. People & Influence (1-5)
- **1 — Poor:** No evidence of people skills or influence ability. Self-focused.
- **2 — Below Average:** Acknowledges people aspects but lacks concrete examples of effective leadership.
- **3 — Average:** Shows some people awareness. Can describe leading situations but impact is unclear.
- **4 — Good:** Strong people orientation. Clear examples of developing others, resolving conflicts, and building trust.
- **5 — Excellent:** Exceptional people leadership. Demonstrates empathy, ability to inspire, thoughtful approach to talent development, and creating psychological safety.

### 4. Overall Score (1-5)
- Would you trust this person to lead a team or initiative?
- Do they balance results with people?
- Do they show growth and self-awareness as a leader?
