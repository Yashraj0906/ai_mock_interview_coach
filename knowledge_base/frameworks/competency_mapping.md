# Competency Mapping Framework

## Purpose

This framework helps the Session Planner map ANY role to the most relevant interview 
question categories. The LLM uses its world knowledge about the role combined with 
this framework to determine which competencies to assess.

## Universal Competency Categories

### 1. Behavioral Competencies
**Applicable to:** ALL roles
**What it assesses:** Past behavior patterns, self-awareness, growth mindset
**Key signals:** STAR-format answers, specific examples, personal accountability
**When to prioritize:** Always include at least 1-2 behavioral questions regardless of role

### 2. Technical Competencies
**Applicable to:** Roles with domain-specific knowledge requirements
**Examples:** Software engineering, data analysis, design, finance, legal, medical, marketing analytics
**What it assesses:** Depth of domain knowledge, problem-solving approach, tool proficiency
**Key signals:** Technical accuracy, ability to explain trade-offs, practical experience
**When to prioritize:** When the role requires specific technical skills or domain expertise

### 3. Situational/Hypothetical Competencies
**Applicable to:** ALL roles
**What it assesses:** Judgment, decision-making, adaptability, critical thinking
**Key signals:** Structured reasoning, consideration of stakeholders, risk awareness
**When to prioritize:** When assessing how candidates handle ambiguity or novel scenarios

### 4. Case/Analytical Competencies
**Applicable to:** Strategy-heavy roles, consulting, product, business operations
**Examples:** Product management, strategy, consulting, business development, operations
**What it assesses:** Structured problem-solving, quantitative reasoning, business acumen
**Key signals:** Framework usage, estimation ability, strategic thinking
**When to prioritize:** When the role requires analytical or strategic decision-making

### 5. Leadership Competencies
**Applicable to:** Manager and senior roles, team leads, founders
**Examples:** Engineering manager, VP, director, team lead, project manager
**What it assesses:** Team building, strategic vision, decision-making, influence
**Key signals:** Evidence of developing others, handling ambiguity, driving results through people
**When to prioritize:** When the role involves managing people or leading initiatives

### 6. Culture Fit / Values Competencies
**Applicable to:** ALL roles
**What it assesses:** Values alignment, communication style, teamwork, professional maturity
**Key signals:** Self-awareness, empathy, collaboration instinct, growth mindset
**When to prioritize:** Always include 1-2 culture questions, especially for collaborative roles

## Mapping Examples

| Role | Primary Categories | Secondary Categories |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Software Engineer | Technical, Behavioral | Situational, Culture Fit |
| Product Manager | Case/Analytical, Behavioral | Situational, Leadership |
| Data Analyst | Technical, Case/Analytical | Behavioral, Culture Fit |
| UX Designer | Technical, Behavioral | Situational, Culture Fit |
| HR Manager | Behavioral, Leadership | Situational, Culture Fit |
| Marketing Manager | Case/Analytical, Behavioral | Leadership, Culture Fit |
| DevOps Engineer | Technical, Situational | Behavioral, Culture Fit |
| AI Research Intern | Technical, Behavioral | Situational, Culture Fit |
| Sales Representative | Behavioral, Situational | Case/Analytical, Culture Fit |
| Operations Manager | Situational, Leadership | Case/Analytical, Behavioral |

**Note:** These mappings are examples. The Session Planner LLM should determine the optimal 
mapping for any role based on its understanding of the role's requirements.
