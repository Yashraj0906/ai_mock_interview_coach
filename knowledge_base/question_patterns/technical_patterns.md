# Technical Interview Question Patterns

These are universal technical question templates that probe depth of knowledge, 
problem-solving ability, and system thinking. Adapt to ANY role's domain.

## Pattern: System Design & Architecture

### Easy
- "Walk me through how you would design a simple [system/tool/workflow] from scratch. What components would you need?"
- "Explain a technical concept from your domain as if you were teaching it to someone new to the field."

### Medium
- "Describe the architecture of a system you built or contributed to. What were the key design decisions?"
- "If you had to redesign a tool or process you currently use, what would you change and why?"
- "Walk me through how you would approach [domain-specific technical challenge]. What trade-offs would you consider?"

### Hard
- "Describe a situation where you had to make a significant technical trade-off. What factors did you weigh, and how did you validate your decision?"
- "How would you design a system that needs to handle [scale/reliability/performance] requirements? Walk me through your approach from requirements gathering to implementation."

## Pattern: Debugging & Troubleshooting

### Easy
- "Tell me about a bug or issue you found and fixed. How did you identify the root cause?"
- "Describe your typical approach when something isn't working as expected."

### Medium
- "Walk me through a complex debugging process you went through. What tools and techniques did you use?"
- "Tell me about a time when the obvious solution to a technical problem turned out to be wrong. What was actually going on?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to debug a problem in a system you weren't familiar with."

### Hard
- "Tell me about the most difficult technical issue you've ever resolved. What made it challenging, and what was your systematic approach?"
- "Describe a production incident you were involved in. How did you diagnose it under pressure, and what did you implement to prevent it from happening again?"

## Pattern: Technical Decision-Making

### Easy
- "How do you decide which tool or technology to use for a given task?"
- "Tell me about a technology you evaluated recently. What was your assessment?"

### Medium
- "Describe a situation where you had to choose between two valid technical approaches. What criteria did you use to decide?"
- "Tell me about a time you advocated for a specific technical approach. How did you make your case?"
- "How do you balance technical debt against feature development? Give me a specific example."

### Hard
- "Describe a time when you had to make a technical decision that would have long-term implications. How did you evaluate the risks and trade-offs?"
- "Tell me about a technical bet you made that didn't pay off. What would you do differently?"

## Pattern: Domain Knowledge Deep-Dive

### Easy
- "What are the key principles or best practices in your area of expertise?"
- "How do you stay current with developments in your field?"

### Medium
- "What's a common misconception in your domain, and what's the reality?"
- "If you could change one thing about how your domain is typically practiced, what would it be and why?"
- "Describe how a recent industry development has affected or could affect your work."

### Hard
- "What do you think will be the most significant change in your field over the next 2-3 years? How are you preparing for it?"
- "Take me through a complex concept in your domain. Help me understand not just what it is, but why it matters and what the alternatives are."
