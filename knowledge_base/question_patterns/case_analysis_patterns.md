# Case Analysis Interview Question Patterns

These are business case and problem-solving question templates that test analytical 
thinking, structured reasoning, and strategic judgment. Adaptable to ANY role.

## Pattern: Business Problem Analysis

### Easy
- "A key metric in your area has dropped 15% over the past month. Walk me through how you would investigate the root cause."
- "If you were asked to improve a process that your team uses daily, how would you approach it?"

### Medium
- "Your team launches a new initiative, and early results are mixed — some metrics improving, others declining. How do you evaluate whether to continue, pivot, or stop?"
- "You're given a budget to solve a problem but the budget only covers 60% of the ideal solution. How do you decide what to cut?"
- "A competitor just launched a feature/product/service that overlaps with your team's work. How do you assess the impact and respond?"

### Hard
- "You're asked to build a business case for a significant investment in your domain. Walk me through your approach, from identifying the opportunity to presenting to leadership."
- "A strategic bet your team made 6 months ago isn't showing results yet. Leadership wants to know if they should continue investing. How do you frame your analysis?"

## Pattern: Estimation & Quantitative Reasoning

### Easy
- "How would you estimate the number of [relevant items] in [relevant context]? Walk me through your thinking."
- "If you had to measure the success of your team's work, what metrics would you track and why?"

### Medium
- "How would you design an experiment to test whether a proposed change would actually improve [relevant outcome]?"
- "You need to forecast [domain-specific metric] for the next quarter. What data would you need, and what approach would you take?"
- "Walk me through how you would measure the ROI of [a relevant initiative or tool]."

### Hard
- "You have conflicting data from two reliable sources pointing to different conclusions. How do you reconcile them and make a recommendation?"
- "Design a framework for evaluating whether your team should build, buy, or partner for a new capability."

## Pattern: Strategy & Product Thinking

### Easy
- "If you could add one feature or capability to a tool/product you use daily, what would it be and why?"
- "Who do you think is the target audience for [relevant product/service], and what do they care about most?"

### Medium
- "How would you prioritize between improving existing capabilities versus building new ones? What framework would you use?"
- "You're asked to define the roadmap for your area for the next 6 months. Walk me through your process."
- "A user/customer complains that your product/service doesn't solve their problem. How do you investigate and respond?"

### Hard
- "You're entering a market/area where competitors have a significant head start. What would your strategy be?"
- "How would you evaluate the long-term viability of a technology, tool, or approach your team depends on?"
