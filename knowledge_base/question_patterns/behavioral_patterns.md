# Behavioral Interview Question Patterns

These are universal behavioral question templates using the STAR method framework. 
They can be adapted to ANY role by substituting role-specific context.

## Pattern: Leadership & Influence

### Easy
- "Tell me about a time you had to convince someone to see things your way. What approach did you take?"
- "Describe a situation where you took the lead on a project. What was the outcome?"

### Medium
- "Tell me about a time you had to influence a decision without having formal authority. How did you approach it?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to rally a team around a challenging goal. What strategies did you use?"
- "Give me an example of when you had to lead through ambiguity — the path forward wasn't clear, but you had to move the team anyway."

### Hard
- "Tell me about a time you made an unpopular decision. How did you handle the pushback, and would you make the same decision again?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to lead a cross-functional initiative with competing priorities. How did you align stakeholders?"

## Pattern: Conflict Resolution

### Easy
- "Tell me about a disagreement you had with a colleague. How did you resolve it?"
- "Describe a time when you received critical feedback. How did you respond?"

### Medium
- "Give me an example of a time two team members had a significant disagreement and you had to step in. What did you do?"
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager's direction. How did you handle it?"
- "Describe a situation where a project was going off track due to interpersonal issues. What steps did you take?"

### Hard
- "Tell me about a time you had to deliver difficult feedback to someone more senior than you. How did you approach it?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to manage a conflict between two teams with fundamentally different goals. What was your approach and what trade-offs did you make?"

## Pattern: Problem-Solving Under Pressure

### Easy
- "Tell me about a time you faced an unexpected problem at work. How did you handle it?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to meet a tight deadline. What did you do?"

### Medium
- "Give me an example of a time you identified a problem before anyone else noticed it. What did you do?"
- "Tell me about a situation where your initial approach to a problem failed. How did you pivot?"
- "Describe a time when you had to make a quick decision with incomplete information. What was your process?"

### Hard
- "Tell me about the most complex problem you've solved professionally. Walk me through your thinking process."
- "Describe a situation where you were responsible for a significant mistake. How did you handle it, and what systems did you put in place to prevent recurrence?"

## Pattern: Teamwork & Collaboration

### Easy
- "Tell me about a successful team project you worked on. What was your role?"
- "Describe a time you helped a teammate who was struggling."

### Medium
- "Give me an example of a time you worked with someone whose working style was very different from yours. How did you adapt?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to collaborate across departments to deliver a result."
- "Describe a project where the team dynamic was challenging. What did you do to improve it?"

### Hard
- "Tell me about a time you had to build trust with a new team quickly. What specific actions did you take?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to balance individual accountability with team success. How did you navigate that tension?"

## Pattern: Growth & Learning

### Easy
- "Tell me about a skill you recently learned. What motivated you to learn it?"
- "Describe a time you received feedback that changed how you work."

### Medium
- "Give me an example of a time you had to learn something completely new to succeed in a project. How did you approach it?"
- "Tell me about a professional failure. What did you learn from it?"

### Hard
- "Tell me about a time you had to fundamentally change your approach to work based on new information or changing circumstances."
- "Describe the biggest gap between your expectations and reality in a professional context. How did you adapt?"
