# Leadership Interview Question Patterns

These are leadership and management question templates that test decision-making,
team development, vision, and influence. Adaptable to ANY leadership or senior role.

## Pattern: Team Building & Development

### Easy
- "How do you approach onboarding a new team member? What do you prioritize in their first week?"
- "Describe your management or leadership style. How do you adapt it to different situations?"

### Medium
- "How do you identify and develop high-potential individuals on your team?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to give difficult performance feedback. How did you handle it, and what was the outcome?"
- "How do you create an environment where people feel safe to take risks and make mistakes?"

### Hard
- "You inherit a team with low morale and unclear direction. Walk me through your first 90 days."
- "How do you balance developing junior team members (which is slower) with the need to deliver results quickly?"

## Pattern: Strategic Thinking & Vision

### Easy
- "Where do you think your field/industry is heading, and how should teams prepare?"
- "How do you set goals for yourself or your team? What frameworks do you use?"

### Medium
- "How do you translate a broad organizational goal into specific, actionable work for your team?"
- "Describe how you would evaluate whether your team is working on the right things."
- "How do you balance short-term wins with long-term strategic goals?"

### Hard
- "You're asked to define the vision for a new function/team/initiative. Walk me through how you'd approach it."
- "How do you make decisions when the long-term strategic direction conflicts with short-term business needs?"

## Pattern: Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

### Easy
- "How do you typically make decisions when you don't have all the information?"
- "What's your approach to risk assessment?"

### Medium
- "Describe a time when you had to make a significant decision with incomplete data. What was your process?"
- "How do you decide when to commit to a direction versus when to gather more information?"
- "When multiple approaches seem equally valid, how do you break the tie?"

### Hard
- "You're facing a decision that could have significant positive upside but also substantial downside risk. How do you evaluate and decide?"
- "Describe a situation where you had to reverse a previous decision. How did you handle it with your team and stakeholders?"

## Pattern: Change Management

### Easy
- "How do you stay adaptable when priorities change?"
- "What's your approach when you need to implement a change that affects multiple people?"

### Medium
- "How do you drive adoption of a new tool, process, or way of working within your team?"
- "Describe how you've handled resistance to change. What worked and what didn't?"
- "How do you communicate difficult changes (reorgs, pivots, cutbacks) to your team?"

### Hard
- "You're tasked with transforming a team's culture from reactive to proactive. How do you approach this multi-year initiative?"
- "A major strategic pivot requires your team to abandon work they've been deeply invested in. How do you manage the transition while maintaining engagement?"
